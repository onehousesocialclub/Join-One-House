Privacy Notice

One House Social Club ("we", "us", "our") takes your privacy seriously. This notice explains what personal data we collect when you sign up, how we use it, and your rights under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

One House Social Club is a London-based social membership community. We are the data controller for the personal information you provide. For any privacy queries, contact us at onehousesocialclub@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

Membership sign-up: We collect your first name, last name, email address, Instagram handle, date of birth, and how you heard about us. We use this to set up and manage your membership, verify eligibility, and personalise your experience.

Marketing communications: With your consent, we'll send you event announcements, member news, and what's on. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Event attendance: If you book events through Ticket Tailor, we may record attendance for operational and safety purposes.

Our lawful basis

We process your data on the basis of contract (to manage your membership), consent (for marketing emails), and legitimate interests (to improve our events and community). We will never use your data in ways you wouldn't reasonably expect.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the following trusted processors who help us run One House:

Disciple Media - our community app platform (onehousesocialc.disciplemedia.com)

Ticket Tailor - our event ticketing platform

Both operate under data processing agreements and are bound by UK GDPR. We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect the safety of our members.

How long we keep your data

We retain your personal data for as long as your membership is active. If you cancel or we close your account, we'll retain your data for up to 12 months for administrative purposes, then securely delete it - unless we're required to keep it longer by law.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to: access your data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion ("right to be forgotten"), object to processing, restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email onehousesocialclub@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and tracking

This signup page does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses only essential functionality to render the page correctly. No third-party ad networks receive your data from this page.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page. We'll notify active members of any material changes by email.
